### 🌱 About me

- 🎓 MSc in **Metaverse Technology** at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University — named to the **Dean's Honours List** (top 5% of the programme)
- 🏆 **Champion of COMPUTHON 2026**, postgraduate stream, competing solo as team *"Anti-DDL"*
- 🛠️ I build **XR and game client software** in Unity and C#, and I like the parts where graphics, networking and state machines meet
- 🤖 **AI-assisted development is my default workflow** — *BinaryPathway* was built with an AI agent driving the Unity Editor over MCP, and I've wired LLMs into my own services (DeepSeek decision controller, ComfyUI generation pipelines). I know enough about how these models work to debug them, not just prompt them
- 🧩 Recent obsession: rebuilding small games from games I love — usually with a solver bolted on
- 🌏 Shenyang, China · 中文 / English · happy to talk about VR, generative 3D, or puzzle design
